FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Every question, explanation, and study guide on this site is free, with no account required. We also sell an optional Premium Pack of extra mock exams, but every practice test, explanation, and study guide on this site stays free.

How many questions are on the real Part 107 exam?

60 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes at a PSI testing center. You need 70% to pass — that means you can miss up to 18 questions. Our mock exam mirrors this format and draws its 60 questions by the FAA's current area weighting (the blueprint in effect since September 29, 2025).

Are these the actual FAA exam questions?

No one outside the FAA has the live exam bank — anyone claiming otherwise is guessing. Our questions are original, written to the same specifications the FAA publishes: the Remote Pilot Airman Certification Standards, 14 CFR Part 107, and the FAA study guides. Every explanation cites its source.

How do I know when I'm ready?

Our rule of thumb (not an FAA standard): score 85% or higher on two consecutive full mock exams. That leaves a 15-point cushion over the 70% pass mark, though no practice score guarantees a pass. The cost & difficulty guide includes a free 2-week study plan.
