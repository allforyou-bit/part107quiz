Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free?

Yes. Every question, explanation, and study guide on this site is free, with no account required. We may add optional paid study materials later, but the practice tests stay free.

How many questions are on the real Part 107 exam?

60 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes at a PSI testing center. You need 70% to pass — that means you can miss up to 18 questions. Our mock exam mirrors this format, including the FAA's published topic weighting.

Are these the actual FAA exam questions?

No one outside the FAA has the live exam bank — anyone claiming otherwise is guessing. Our questions are original, written to the same specifications the FAA publishes: the Remote Pilot Airman Certification Standards, 14 CFR Part 107, and the FAA study guides. Every explanation cites its source.

How do I know when I'm ready?

A common benchmark: score 85% or higher on two consecutive full mock exams. That buffer comfortably clears the 70% pass mark even with test-day nerves. The cost & difficulty guide includes a free 2-week study plan.
