Part 107 Weather Practice Test

Weather is 11–16% of the exam, and at least one raw METAR usually shows up expecting you to decode it cold. These 15 questions cover the visibility and cloud-clearance minimums, METAR/TAF decoding, density altitude, and storm behavior. For the full walkthrough, read Aviation Weather for Drone Pilots.
