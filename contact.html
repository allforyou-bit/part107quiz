Reporting an error in a question

This is the message we most want to receive. Our questions are written against the FAA's published sources, but regulations change and writers make mistakes. If a question, an answer, or an explanation looks wrong to you, email us with:

  • the page it appears on (for example, the regulations practice test)
  • the question text, or enough of it that we can find it
  • what you believe the correct answer is, and why

If you can point to the CFR section, ACS line, or FAA handbook page that supports your reading, include it — that turns a correction into a five-minute fix. Confirmed corrections ship the same week, and the whole written question set on each topic page is regenerated from a single source file, so a fix lands everywhere at once.

Questions about the exam itself

We are happy to point you at the right material, but we cannot act as your certifying authority. Anything binding — your eligibility, your test appointment, your certificate status, whether a specific operation needs a waiver — has to come from the FAA or from PSI, not from us. Start with the FAA's own drone pages at faa.gov/uas. For how the process works in plain English, our step-by-step licensing guide covers IACRA registration, scheduling, and what test day looks like.

Suggesting a topic

The study guide library grows every week, and reader requests drive a good share of it. If there is a subject you keep getting wrong and cannot find a clear explanation for, tell us — that is usually a sign the explanation does not exist anywhere yet, which makes it worth writing.

Reuse, permissions, and press

Our questions, explanations, and study guides are our own work and are covered by the site terms. Flight schools and instructors ask fairly often about using material with a class: short answer, ask us first and the answer is usually yes with attribution. Bulk copying to a competing site is not something we license. For interviews or citations, the same address reaches us.

Privacy

Emails you send us are used only to answer you. We do not add correspondents to a mailing list, sell addresses, or share them with anyone. Our full privacy policy covers what the site itself does and does not collect — the short version is that the quizzes run entirely in your browser and your answers never leave your device.
