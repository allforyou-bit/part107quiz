Part 107 Mock Exam

Exactly like test day: 60 questions drawn with the FAA's published area weighting (Regulations 15–25%, Airspace 15–25%, Weather 11–16%, Loading & Performance 7–11%, Operations 35–45%), a 120-minute countdown, and no feedback until you finish — then a full per-area breakdown and explanations for every miss. A fresh question mix is drawn each attempt.

Tip: treat your first mock as a diagnostic. Whatever area scores lowest, read its study guide and drill its topic test before your next attempt. When you can score 85%+ twice in a row, you have a comfortable buffer over the real 70% pass mark.
