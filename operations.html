Part 107 Operations Practice Test

Operations is the biggest block on the exam at 35–45%: the five hazardous attitudes and their exact antidotes, IMSAFE, crew resource management, hyperventilation and night vision, CTAF radio calls, lost-link and battery-fire emergencies. These 26 questions cover the patterns the FAA loves to test. Memorize the antidotes word-for-word — the exam rewards it.

Operations questions also draw on night operations and Remote ID — finish those two short drills, then run the full mock exam.
